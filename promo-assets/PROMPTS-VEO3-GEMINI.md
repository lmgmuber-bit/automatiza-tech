# Prompts para Veo 3 (Google Gemini) — Video TECH Hablando
> Videos de 8 segundos | TECH habla directo a camara | Colores corporativos AT

---

## COMO USAR EN VEO 3

1. Ve a aistudio.google.com y selecciona Veo 3
2. SUBE LA IMAGEN DE TECH (robot con fondo circular turquesa) como referencia — esto es clave
3. Pega el prompt elegido tal como esta
4. Configura: 8 segundos, 16:9, Con audio activado

## PROMPT 1 — TECH Presenta la Promo (PRINCIPAL / RECOMENDADO)

Create an 8-second cinematic promotional video of a friendly 3D animated robot named "TECH" speaking directly to camera. Use the uploaded reference image for the robot design.

ROBOT APPEARANCE (match reference image exactly):
- Dark navy blue metallic body (#1e3a8a)
- Glowing teal/turquoise accent lines and panels (#06d6a0, #17b7b1)
- Dark visor face with two large bright CYAN LED glowing eyes and a LED smile
- Small glowing teal antenna on top of round head
- Headphones on sides of head
- Circular "AT" logo badge on chest, white lettering
- Compact friendly proportions, polished metallic surface

SCENE:
- TECH stands center-frame on a dark navy blue background (#0a1628 to #1e3a8a gradient)
- Subtle teal circuit board patterns glow softly behind robot
- Floating teal particles drift upward throughout
- Soft cinematic teal rim lighting from below

ANIMATION (8 seconds):
- 0-1s: TECH appears with subtle entrance, waves hello, eyes blink
- 1-5s: TECH speaks with natural head movement and small hand gestures, eyes glow brighter at key words
- 5-7s: Orange glowing badge "1 MES GRATIS" appears floating beside TECH, antenna pulses
- 7-8s: "automatizatech.cl" fades in at bottom in teal, TECH gives thumbs up

NARRATION — TECH speaks in Spanish, warm friendly male voice with subtle robotic filter:
"Hola! Soy TECH de AutomatizaTech. Conecta WhatsApp, Instagram y todos tus canales en una sola plataforma con inteligencia artificial. Tu primer mes es completamente gratis!"

AUDIO: Subtle upbeat electronic corporate background music (low volume), TECH's voice is clear and prominent.

STYLE: Cinematic 4K, smooth 3D animation, dark navy and teal color palette, modern tech corporate feel.

## PROMPT 2 — TECH Senala los Planes

Create an 8-second video featuring a 3D robot mascot "TECH" presenting pricing plans. Use reference image for robot design.

ROBOT: Navy blue metallic body with teal glowing accents (#06d6a0), cyan LED eyes on dark visor, "AT" chest badge, antenna. Match reference image exactly.

SCENE FLOW:
- Seconds 0-2: TECH stands left-of-center, turns to camera with a greeting wave
- Seconds 2-6: TECH gestures to the right where two holographic pricing cards appear with teal glow:
  Card 1: "Basico" "$99/mes" orange badge "1 MES GRATIS"
  Card 2 (larger, glowing teal border, "MAS POPULAR" badge): "Profesional" "$199/mes" orange badge "1 MES GRATIS"
  TECH points at each card as it appears, eyes lighting up
- Seconds 6-8: TECH faces camera, gives thumbs up, "automatizatech.cl" appears in teal

NARRATION (Spanish, friendly robotic male voice):
"En AutomatizaTech tenemos el plan perfecto para ti! Plan Basico desde noventa y nueve dolares, o el Profesional por ciento noventa y nueve. Ambos con el primer mes gratis!"

BACKGROUND: Dark navy to royal blue gradient, floating teal particles, faint circuit grid.
AUDIO: Corporate electronic background music, clear narration voice.
STYLE: Modern tech, cinematic, 4K, smooth animation.

## PROMPT 3 — TECH con Canales Orbitando (Dinamico)

Create an 8-second dynamic video of robot "TECH" surrounded by floating channel icons. Use reference image for robot appearance.

ROBOT: Dark navy blue body, teal accent lines, cyan LED eyes, AT chest badge. Friendly and expressive.

SCENE:
- Seconds 0-1: TECH appears centered, eyes glow, arms slightly open
- Seconds 1-5: Five holographic social media channel icons orbit around TECH in a circular path:
  WhatsApp icon (green #25D366 glow)
  Instagram icon (pink to purple gradient glow)
  Telegram icon (blue #229ED9 glow)
  Messenger icon (blue #0084FF glow)
  Email envelope icon (white glow)
  TECH's head follows the icons rotating, eyes tracking them with curiosity
- Seconds 5-7: All icons fly into TECH's chest badge with a flash of white-teal light. Text appears large: "UNA SOLA PLATAFORMA"
- Seconds 7-8: Orange badge "1 MES GRATIS" pulses. "automatizatech.cl" appears. TECH raises fists in celebration.

NARRATION (Spanish, warm robotic voice):
"WhatsApp, Instagram, Telegram, Messenger, Email... todos en AutomatizaTech! Una sola plataforma inteligente. Pruebala gratis por un mes!"

BACKGROUND: Deep dark navy (#0a1628), teal and blue particle effects, glowing circuit patterns.
AUDIO: Energetic electronic corporate music, clear Spanish narration.
STYLE: Cinematic 4K, dynamic camera movement, modern tech aesthetic.

## PROMPT 5 — Solo Animacion Sin Dialogo (para editar voz despues)

Create an 8-second smooth animation of robot mascot "TECH" from AutomatizaTech. No spoken dialogue. Match reference image for design.

ROBOT: Dark navy blue metallic body with teal glowing accent lines (#06d6a0), large friendly cyan LED glowing eyes on dark visor face, gentle LED smile, small teal glowing antenna on top, headphones, circular "AT" logo badge on chest.

ANIMATION SEQUENCE:
- 0-2s: TECH enters from center with light burst, waves hello, both LED eyes blink
- 2-4s: TECH opens both hands forward in welcoming pose, antenna pulses teal
- 4-6s: TECH points forward at camera, eyes glow brighter, slight lean forward
- 6-8s: TECH gives enthusiastic thumbs up with right hand, small teal spark effects burst around fist

BACKGROUND: Deep dark navy (#0a1628) radial gradient with soft royal blue (#1e3a8a) edges, subtle floating teal (#06d6a0) particles drifting upward, faint glowing circuit board patterns.

LIGHTING: Cinematic soft teal rim light from below, subtle spotlight from above.
AUDIO: Upbeat electronic corporate background music only, no voice.
STYLE: Smooth professional 3D animation, 4K, loop-friendly, no text overlays.
